Course Pathways

Choosing a development pathway

The right timeline depends on how ready your Course is when the project begins.

This page is a planning guide, not a formal scope of work. The CTL Digital Program Development Team will confirm your project's specific timeline and support needs.

Shared assumptions

The Ready to publish, Accelerated, and Standard pathways assume:

  • The Course uses standard formats such as videos, readings, activities, and auto-graded assessments.
  • Faculty have an active role in developing and reviewing the content.
  • Faculty prepare scripts, slides, readings, and other source materials.
  • Slides and other visual materials are accessible and copyright-ready or can be revised within the project.
  • Copyright and accessibility questions can be resolved within the project.
  • The Course does not require complex tools, activities, integrations, or visual design.
  • There are no significant external requirements or multiple levels of review and approval.

These timelines are planning ranges rather than fixed commitments. A longer Course, larger scope, complex production needs, or additional review requirements may require more time.

Approximately 4–6 weeks

Ready to Publish

The timeline below assumes a standard Course of approximately 2–8 hours of learner time, with 2–4 learning objectives and 3–6 Modules. Larger or more complex Courses may require additional time.

This pathway is a good fit if:

  • Your Course content is complete and in good condition.
  • Your materials are already appropriate for the intended Coursera audience.
  • Scripts, slides, readings, activities, and assessments are ready to use.
  • Copyright, permissions, and ownership are clear.
  • Materials meet accessibility expectations or need only minor revisions.
  • The Course does not require substantial redesign or new content development.

The main work is reviewing, building, QA, and preparing the Course for publication.

Review, build, and publish

You'll share your Course content with the Learning Experience team. We'll review the materials, flag any copyright, accessibility, or learner-experience concerns, and identify any final revisions needed before the build. You'll address requested changes while the Learning Experience team builds and reviews the Course in Coursera.

By the end of this stage, you'll have

  • Course content reviewed and ready for publication
  • Copyright and accessibility concerns addressed
  • A completed Course in Coursera
  • A Course submitted for publication

Launch and after

Courses typically go live approximately seven days after submission, depending on Coursera's review process. After launch, the Learning Experience team will monitor initial learner feedback, share relevant notes with you, and respond to learner support needs and technical issues throughout the life of the Course.

Approximately 3 months

Accelerated: Existing Content Is Ready

The timeline below assumes a standard Course of approximately 2–8 hours of learner time, with 2–4 learning objectives and 3–6 Modules. Larger or more complex Courses may require additional time.

This pathway is a good fit if:

  • The Course is relatively short, typically two to six hours of learner time.
  • Most of the needed content already exists.
  • You are familiar with Coursera or online learning design.
  • Existing content is in reasonably good condition but needs reframing for a commercial learning platform.
  • You can prepare or revise scripts, slides, readings, activities, and assessments quickly.
  • Copyright and accessibility issues are limited and manageable.
  • The Course uses standard formats and does not require complex production.

The main work is adapting existing content, filling small gaps, resolving copyright and accessibility needs, building the Course, and preparing it for launch.

Accelerated: The Learning Plan Is Ready

This pathway is a good fit if:

  • The Course is relatively short, typically two to six hours of learner time.
  • You already have clear learning outcomes and a well-developed Course or Module plan.
  • You are familiar with Coursera or online learning design.
  • You are comfortable developing scripts, slides, and other source materials.
  • The main work is creating the Learning Assets identified in the plan.
  • You can make timely decisions and respond to feedback as assets are developed.
  • The Course uses standard formats and does not require complex production.

The main work is developing a demonstration Module, establishing the development process, creating the remaining assets, building the Course, and preparing it for launch.

Approximately 6 months

Standard Development

The timeline below assumes a standard Course of approximately 2–8 hours of learner time, with 2–4 learning objectives and 3–6 Modules. Larger or more complex Courses may require additional time. This is the baseline Course development process.

This pathway is a good fit if:

  • You have limited Coursera or online learning experience.
  • You have little existing source content to adapt.
  • The Course needs meaningful support with learning objectives, Course structure, Module design, and Learning Asset planning.
  • You are willing to develop scripts, slides, and other source materials with guidance.
  • The Course uses standard formats and does not require complex production.
  • The Course can be developed without significant external review or approval requirements.

Your Learning Experience Designer will guide you through each stage, while you remain the subject-matter expert and primary content author.

Approximately 6+ months

Custom Development

The timeline below assumes a standard Course of approximately 2–8 hours of learner time, with 2–4 learning objectives and 3–6 Modules. Larger or more complex Courses may require additional time. Custom Courses may fall outside this typical structure — the team will work with you to define the appropriate scope, learner time, number of Modules, and Learning Assets.

A Course may require a custom timeline when the standard pathway assumptions do not apply. This pathway is a good fit if:

  • The Course needs continuing education or another formal review process.
  • The Course must meet grant, accreditation, or other external requirements.
  • The project requires multiple levels of stakeholder review or approval.
  • The Course begins with an idea that needs substantial development.
  • The project requires significant pedagogical or curriculum support.
  • The Course includes complex videos, activities, tools, integrations, or visual design.
  • Faculty need substantial support developing scripts, slides, or other source materials.
  • The Course is longer or substantially larger than the standard Course model.
  • The project includes other requirements that may affect the development process or timeline.

Custom projects may include additional planning, consultation, co-design, media production, visual design, accessibility review, stakeholder review, or platform testing.

A tailored process — the Learning Experience team will work with you to define

  • The Course's learning goals and scope
  • The level of design and development support needed
  • The content and media production plan
  • The review, approval, and sign-off process
  • The timeline and major milestones
  • The roles and responsibilities of everyone involved

The Course may still use the Coursera Design Guide, but the team will identify which sections and resources are most relevant to the project. The timeline will be developed around the Course's specific needs — a custom Course may take six months or longer to complete.

Throughout development, you will remain the subject-matter expert and primary content author unless the project scope establishes a different arrangement. The Learning Experience team will help coordinate the process, provide design and platform expertise, and support the Course through development, review, and launch.
