02 · What development looks like

From idea to launch

Most projects move through the same general stages. Where your project begins, and how much time each stage needs, depends on how ready your content is. That's what the development pathways describe.

1

Define

Clarify who the learners are and what they should be able to do.

2

Plan

Organize the learning into Courses and Modules and identify the Learning Assets needed.

3

Develop

Prepare scripts, slides, readings, activities, and assessments, often starting with a demonstration Module.

4

Build and review

CTL builds and QAs the experience in Coursera. Faculty review and provide final sign-off.

5

Launch and support

Content typically goes live about seven days after submission. CTL monitors learner feedback and support needs.

03 · How we share the work

Faculty and the CTL Design Team

You bring the subject-matter expertise and remain the primary content author. The CTL Design Team helps shape the learning experience and prepares it for Coursera.

As faculty, you will typically

  • Define and confirm the purpose of the Course or Specialization
  • Identify the learners you hope to serve
  • Confirm what learners should be able to do by the end
  • Provide or develop the content
  • Prepare scripts, slides, readings, activities, assessments, and projects
  • Review feedback and make content decisions
  • Confirm that the final experience is accurate and aligned with your goals
  • Review and approve the experience before it is submitted to Coursera

The CTL Design Team will help you

  • Translate your teaching expertise into an online learning experience
  • Plan the Specialization, Course, Module, and Learning Asset structure
  • Support decisions about sequencing and learner progression
  • Provide guidance on learning design, accessibility, and copyright
  • Review and refine content as it is developed
  • Build and QA the experience in Coursera
  • Monitor learner feedback and support needs after launch
